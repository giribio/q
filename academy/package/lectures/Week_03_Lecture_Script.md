# Week 03: Quantum states and gates

## Instructor lecture script

Explain gates as unitary transformations. Derive X, Y and Z as Pauli matrices and show their action on basis states. Derive the Hadamard gate and explain phase. Introduce S, T and rotation gates. Use the Bloch sphere as a geometric representation of pure one-qubit states. Ask learners to verify unitarity numerically.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
- End with a 3-question retrieval-practice quiz.

## Key equations and concepts

- State update: `|ψ'⟩ = U|ψ⟩`
- Hadamard: `H = (1/√2)[[1,1],[1,-1]]`
