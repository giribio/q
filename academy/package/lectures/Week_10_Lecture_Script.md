# Week 10: Quantum materials

## Instructor lecture script

Introduce spin Hamiltonians and the Ising model. Derive a two-spin transverse-field Ising Hamiltonian using tensor products of Pauli operators. Show exact diagonalisation as a classical baseline. Discuss ground-state energy and finite-size effects. Connect QAOA and variational methods to simplified lattice models, but avoid implying that a tiny spin model predicts real materials directly.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
