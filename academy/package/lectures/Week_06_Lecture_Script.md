# Week 06: QFT and phase estimation

## Instructor lecture script

Motivate the Fourier transform as a change of basis. Derive the discrete QFT matrix and show its unitarity. Explain controlled phase rotations and inverse QFT. Introduce eigenvectors of a unitary and show how phase estimation encodes an eigenphase into a register. Connect QPE to order finding and quantum chemistry, while noting precision and resource requirements.
