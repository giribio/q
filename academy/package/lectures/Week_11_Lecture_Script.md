# Week 11: Noise and hardware

## Instructor lecture script

Describe physical qubits and the sources of noise. Define relaxation and dephasing times T1 and T2 conceptually. Introduce depolarising and amplitude-damping channels and measurement error. Compare ideal and noisy circuit results. Distinguish error mitigation from quantum error correction and fault tolerance. Explain why hardware results require calibration metadata and repeated experiments.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
- End with a 3-question retrieval-practice quiz.
