# Week 02: Mathematical foundations

## Instructor lecture script

Start with complex numbers and vector notation. Derive inner products, norms and normalization. Introduce Dirac notation and translate between ket notation and column vectors. Explain tensor products as the mathematical mechanism for combining subsystems. Demonstrate unitarity and the Born rule with NumPy. Emphasise that all physical quantum gates preserve state norm.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
- End with a 3-question retrieval-practice quiz.

## Key equations and concepts

- Inner product: `⟨u|v⟩`
- Tensor product: `|ψ⟩ ⊗ |φ⟩`
- Unitarity: `U†U = I`

## Slide outline
1. Complex numbers and amplitudes
2. Vectors, matrices and Hilbert spaces
3. Bra-ket notation
4. Tensor products
5. Unitary and Hermitian operators
6. Born rule
