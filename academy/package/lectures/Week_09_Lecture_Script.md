# Week 09: Quantum chemistry

## Instructor lecture script

Begin with the electronic Schrödinger equation and Born–Oppenheimer approximation. Explain molecular orbitals and basis sets. Introduce second quantisation and fermionic creation/annihilation operators. Explain why fermionic Hamiltonians must be mapped to qubits, for example with Jordan–Wigner. Describe VQE for molecular ground-state energy and require comparison with classical references using consistent model assumptions.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
