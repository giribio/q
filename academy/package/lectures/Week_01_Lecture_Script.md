# Week 01: Introduction to quantum computing

## Instructor lecture script

Introduce quantum information by contrasting deterministic classical bits with quantum state vectors. Explain that a qubit is represented by amplitudes over basis states, not by a hidden classical bit. Derive the normalization condition and Born rule. Use the notebook to sample outcomes and discuss why measurement gives one result per shot. Close by distinguishing superposition, entanglement and quantum advantage.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
- End with a 3-question retrieval-practice quiz.

## Key equations and concepts

- Qubit: `|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩`
- Normalization: `|α|² + |β|² = 1`
- Born rule: `P(i) = |⟨i|ψ⟩|²`

## Slide outline
1. Classical information and bits
