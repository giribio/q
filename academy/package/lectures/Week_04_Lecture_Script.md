# Week 04: Multi-qubit circuits and entanglement

## Instructor lecture script

Build circuits from registers, gates and measurement. Explain controlled operations using CNOT. Construct a Bell state step by step and derive its state vector. Contrast entangled and separable states. Explain that Bell-state measurement correlations do not permit faster-than-light signalling. Introduce teleportation as transfer of an unknown state using shared entanglement and classical communication.

## Suggested delivery
- Recorded lecture segments: 5–6 videos of 8–12 minutes each.
- Total recorded time: approximately 60–75 minutes for this week.
- Include a short pause for a worked example after each major concept.
- End with a 3-question retrieval-practice quiz.

## Key equations and concepts

- Bell state: `(|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2`
- Controlled operation: `|c,t⟩ → |c,t ⊕ c⟩` for CNOT.

## Slide outline
